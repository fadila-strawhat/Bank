Frequently Asked Questions

The steps to open a Savings Account are simple. First, you need to visit our website and click on the "Open an Account" button. Then, follow the instructions to create your account. Once your account is created, you can start saving your money and earning interest.

To apply for a Loan, you need to have an active Savings Account with us. Once you have an account, you can log in to your account and navigate to the "Loans" section. From there, you can fill out the application form and submit it. Our team will review your application and contact you with the next steps.

To apply for a Debit Card, you need to have an active Savings Account with us. Once you have an account, you can log in to your account and navigate to the "Debit Cards" section. From there, you can fill out the application form and submit it. Our team will review your application and contact you with the next steps.
